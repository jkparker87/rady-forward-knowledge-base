# User Personas

## Overview

A user persona is a fictional but research-based profile the represents different types of users of our data products. User personas help to clarify who you are designing for and provide a point of reference throughout the development process to make sure you're designing with the user in mind.

They are organized by **role** and segmented by **data literacy level** to highlight how proficiency influences user needs, behaviors, and expectations when interacting with data, analytics, and reporting tools.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 👔 Executive | Strategic decision makers |
| 📊 Director | Leaders driving performance improvement |
| ⚙️ Manager | Operational decision makers |
| 🧠 Data Practitioner | Analytics professionals and builders |
| 🔍 Data Explorer | Researchers, clinicians, and inquisitive users |


### Data Literacy Levels

| Level | Description |
|--------------|------------|
| 🟢**Novice**  | Relies on guided experiences and basic reporting. Limited confidence with data exploration and interpretation. |
| 🟡**Proficient**    | Comfortable exploring data independently, using self-service tools, and performing moderate analysis. |
| 🔵**Advanced** | Highly data literate, capable of complex analysis, advanced exploration, and leveraging sophisticated analytical capabilities. |

# 👔 Executive

## 🟢 Data Literacy Level: Novice

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks clear, high-level insights that support timely decision-making without requiring deep data exploration. Motivated by clarity, efficiency, and confidence in decision-making while minimizing uncertainty and confusion.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs intuitive dashboards, guided insights, clear metric definitions, and streamlined access to information. Struggles with data overload, inconsistent reporting, unclear terminology, and the risk of misinterpreting results.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Typically reviews dashboards periodically and relies on analysts for deeper analysis. Prefers concise summaries over detailed exploration and values simplicity, clarity, and reassurance when interpreting data.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Comfortably reviews dashboards and interprets basic KPIs. Can apply simple filters but avoids more complex interactions. Has a limited understanding of data structures and metric definitions and depends on analysts for advanced insights and interpretation.

## 🟡 Data Literacy Level: Proficient

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks to validate strategy, compare trends, and monitor KPIs with enough detail to confirm performance and inform decisions. Motivated by accuracy, efficiency, and confidence in challenging assumptions through reliable, data-driven insights.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs customizable views, drill-down capabilities, and responsive tools that support efficient exploration. Frustrated by slow dashboards, limited flexibility, and manual processes that hinder analysis and decision-making.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Regularly reviews KPIs, explores dashboards, asks focused questions, and incorporates data into discussions and decision-making. Motivated by curiosity and continuous learning, with a proactive, data-driven approach to problem solving.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Comfortably navigates dashboards, applies filters, explores trends, and investigates underlying drivers through drill-down analysis. Understands metric calculations, data relationships, and basic data preparation activities, with confidence using tools such as Qlik and Excel.

## 🔵 Data Literacy Level: Advanced

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks to drive organizational transformation using predictive analytics and automation. Motivated by innovation, autonomy, and intelligence.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs advanced analytics and predictive models. Pain points include IT bottlenecks, poor data quality, and limited automation.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Self-serves data, builds custom views, integrates multiple sources, and challenges assumptions. Confident, expects complexity, and leads a data-driven culture.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Highly experienced with analytical tools. Expert at interpreting complex dashboards. Understands metric logic. Uses predictive analytics if built within a tool. Acts as a power user and internal data champion.

# 📊 Director

## 🟢 Data Literacy Level: Novice

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks visibility into performance across departments and service lines to identify opportunities for improvement and support organizational objectives. Motivated by using data to inform decisions, communicate priorities to leadership and staff, justify resource needs, and guide improvement initiatives.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs access to meaningful comparisons across teams, departments, and organizational areas, along with guidance on the most valuable views and metrics. Manages many competing priorities and becomes frustrated when data lacks context, actionable insights, or a clear connection to next steps.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Regularly reviews performance data to identify trends, evaluate opportunities, and support decision-making. Often focuses quickly on potential causes and solutions and values data that can be segmented by department, service, unit, time period, or other operational dimensions to support deeper investigation.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Comfortably interprets dashboards and performance measures across multiple areas of the organization. Understands key metrics and trends but relies on analysts for advanced analysis, data validation, and report development. Prefers streamlined reporting experiences with minimal manual filtering or manipulation.

## 🟡 Data Literacy Level: Proficient

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks cross-functional performance insights that reveal trends, variations, and opportunities for improvement across departments and the organization. Motivated by driving improvement initiatives, communicating priorities to leadership, securing resources, and providing transparency around key decisions.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs flexible views that support multiple priorities and evolving business questions. Values actionable insights and meaningful context and becomes frustrated when data lacks a clear connection to decisions, outcomes, or next steps.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Quickly connects data to actions, opportunities, and potential root causes, often focusing on solutions early in the analytical process. Engages most effectively with data that can be explored by department, unit, service, time period, or other meaningful operational segments.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Benefits from clear interpretation and business context when reviewing data while also gaining value from more advanced functionality as data literacy grows. Comfortable exploring alternate measures, additional dimensions, and drill-down capabilities.

## 🔵 Data Literacy Level: Advanced

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks visibility into performance across departments, service lines, and teams to identify variation, uncover opportunities for improvement, and support organizational goals. Motivated by leading improvement initiatives, communicating priorities, advocating for resources, and understanding performance gaps.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs flexible, actionable views of data that support multiple priorities and responsibilities. Frustrated by data that lacks context, meaning, or clear implications and consistently seeks insights that explain why results matter and what actions should follow.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Connects insights directly to operational next steps and root-cause analysis. Benefits from detailed, segmented views that support investigation and validation prior to conclusions.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Values self-service capabilities and independently answers emerging questions while partnering with subject matter experts for deeper analysis. Benefits from broad access to data and responsive support when new opportunities for exploration arise.

# ⚙️ Manager

## 🟢 Data Literacy Level: Novice

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks quick, reliable answers to operational questions related to staffing, patient flow, scheduling, and resource utilization. Motivated by saving time, reducing manual effort, and supporting effective day-to-day decision-making.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs simple dashboards, clear metric definitions, and a trusted source of accurate information. Can feel overwhelmed by complex tools, lengthy reports, and inconsistent data sources and often relies on analysts for basic data requests.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Uses data primarily to address immediate operational needs and solve current challenges. Prefers visual summaries over detailed tables and is generally hesitant to customize reports or dashboards without guidance.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Has basic familiarity with Excel, operational reporting tools, and dashboard navigation. Can access and review available information but rarely creates or customizes reports. Has limited knowledge of statistical concepts and advanced analytical techniques.

## 🟡 Data Literacy Level: Proficient

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks to monitor operational performance across measures such as length of stay, throughput, cancellations, and provider productivity. Motivated by improving outcomes, achieving organizational targets, and evaluating performance across providers, locations, service lines, and time periods.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs consistent metric definitions, streamlined reporting processes, and automated delivery of key insights. Frustrated by inefficient workflows, manual data preparation, and the effort required to answer more complex business questions.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Proactively reviews dashboards, shares findings with peers, and applies data to support ongoing performance improvement. Comfortable experimenting with filters, exports, and integrated data sources.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Demonstrates strong proficiency with spreadsheets, pivot tables, and foundational data modeling concepts. Capable of creating custom views within BI tools and interpreting advanced measures such as benchmarks, percentiles, and moving averages.

## 🔵 Data Literacy Level: Advanced

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks comprehensive insights that inform strategic decisions related to capacity planning, operational performance, quality improvement, and care delivery redesign. Motivated by improving outcomes, reducing variation, advancing value-based care, and evaluating future scenarios.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs trusted, highly detailed data with the ability to drill from summary metrics to encounter-level information. Values integrated views across clinical, operational, staffing, and quality domains. Frustrated by fragmented data and complex analytical outputs that lack context.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Uses analytics as a core leadership capability. Routinely evaluates methodology, assumptions, and data quality before drawing conclusions. Actively embraces new analytical approaches and tools.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Demonstrates strong data literacy and understanding of analytical methodologies and model limitations. Frequently leverages advanced BI functionality and predictive insights to support strategic decisions.

# 🧠 Data Practitioner

## 🟢 Data Literacy Level: Novice

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks to build a strong foundational understanding of data and trends that supports team objectives and organizational goals. Motivated by improving efficiency, reducing manual errors, increasing self-sufficiency, and generating insights.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs clear definitions for metrics, fields, and calculations and repeatable processes for accessing and analyzing data. Can become overwhelmed by ambiguity, complexity, or too many possible approaches.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Relies on structured workflows, templates, and established processes. Regularly validates work with colleagues and approaches challenges with curiosity and a growth mindset.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Has basic proficiency in Excel and simple data manipulation. Uses pre-built dashboards and has limited experience with BI tools, automation, or coding.

## 🟡 Data Literacy Level: Proficient

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks deeper understanding of trends, drivers, and performance patterns to deliver accurate, actionable insights. Motivated by improving efficiency, strengthening data quality, increasing independence, and driving strategic impact.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs documented calculation logic, reliable data access, and sufficient context for repeatable analyses. Frustrated by ambiguity, quality concerns, inefficient workflows, and poor documentation.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Adapts established methods to project-specific needs. Demonstrates curiosity, initiative, and continuous improvement while independently exploring data and validating assumptions.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Demonstrates strong analytical proficiency, including advanced data manipulation, report customization, and foundational coding or SQL skills. Comfortable automating recurring tasks and learning new tools.

## 🔵 Data Literacy Level: Advanced

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks to leverage advanced data sources, analytical methods, and scalable systems to uncover high-impact insights that influence organizational strategy.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs transparency into data sources, transformations, and business logic. Values highly configurable tools that support advanced modeling, automation, integration, and enterprise-wide access to data.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Approaches analytical challenges with independence, curiosity, and a focus on continuous improvement. Proactively identifies opportunities to improve workflows, solve data issues, and uncover patterns.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Demonstrates advanced proficiency in analytics platforms, BI tools, data modeling, automation, and software development practices. Creates scalable solutions, optimized data models, and reusable assets that support a wide audience.

# 🔎 Data Explorer

## 🟢 Data Literacy Level: Novice

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks access to a broad range of data to explore clinical, operational, research, and professional interests. Motivated by curiosity, learning, discovery, and uncovering new insights.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs flexible access to data, measures, and visualizations that support exploratory analysis. Requires clear communication regarding purpose, scope, feasibility, and effort.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Actively explores data, investigates areas of interest, and generates new questions as insights emerge. Benefits from clearly defined objectives and scope boundaries.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Comfortably navigates dashboards and self-service analytics tools. Possesses deep subject matter expertise and prefers flexible visualizations that support exploration and discovery.

## 🟡 Data Literacy Level: Proficient

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks access to operational, clinical, and outcome measures that support exploration, research, and professional development. Motivated by curiosity, discovery, and deep understanding of their field.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs flexibility to explore complex questions while maintaining alignment on scope, priorities, and feasibility. Benefits from transparent communication regarding effort and resource requirements.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Approaches data with curiosity, creativity, and a constant flow of new ideas. Values innovation and collaboration while benefiting from clearly defined objectives.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Prefers highly interactive tools that support extensive filtering and multiple views of information. Benefits from diverse visualizations and flexible analytical experiences.

## 🔵 Data Literacy Level: Advanced

### Goals & Motivation
Seeks access to a broad range of process, outcome, clinical, and research-focused measures. Motivated by advancing knowledge, supporting research activities, and generating new insights.

### Needs & Pain Points
Needs flexible analytical tools that support exploratory analysis, hypothesis generation, and investigation beyond traditional reporting. Values transparency regarding effort, scope, and prioritization.

### Behaviors & Attitudes
Approaches data with intellectual curiosity and frequently generates new questions and opportunities for exploration. Thrives when creativity and innovation are encouraged within a structured framework.

### Skills & Technical Proficiency
Values highly interactive and customizable analytics experiences that support self-directed exploration. Prefers the ability to filter, segment, visualize, and analyze data from multiple perspectives with minimal constraints.
