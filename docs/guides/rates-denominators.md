# Rates and denominators

Most disagreements about rates are disagreements about the denominator.

A rate should answer:

> Among **which eligible population**, how often did **which event** occur?

## Build the denominator first

For a no-show rate, first identify the appointments that were genuinely eligible to show or not show. Only then classify numerator outcomes.

## Denominator checklist

- What records are eligible?
- What statuses are excluded?
- What date determines the reporting period?
- Can one person or encounter appear more than once?
- Are there minimum volume rules?
- Does the denominator change across departments because workflows differ?

Report the numerator and denominator with the rate whenever possible. A rate without its volume context can be misleading.
