# Content domains

A content domain groups data around a real operational workflow rather than a database schema. Domain pages are the preferred starting point for people who know the business question but do not know where the data lives.

Each domain documents:

- the business questions commonly asked
- the major events and states
- important timestamps
- concepts that are frequently confused
- common metrics
- recommended curated entry points
- known interpretation risks

A concept or metric can belong to more than one domain. Those relationships are represented through metadata and automatically cross-linked.
