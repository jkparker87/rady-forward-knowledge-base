# Business concepts

A business concept page explains an idea before it explains a column.

Each concept answers three questions:

1. **What do people usually mean by this?**
2. **How does the operational system represent it?**
3. **How should analysts interpret it?**

This matters because a familiar word can hide several technical representations. “Provider,” for example, may refer to scheduling provider, rendering provider, attending provider, billing provider, or another role. The right answer depends on the question.
