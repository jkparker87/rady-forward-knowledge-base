# Governance

Governance should make trustworthy analysis easier, not add ceremony for its own sake.

This knowledge base separates responsibilities:

- **Business definition:** What does the concept or metric mean?
- **Technical implementation:** How is that meaning represented in curated data?
- **Data stewardship:** Are workflows and definitions being applied consistently?
- **Documentation stewardship:** Is the page current, traceable, and understandable?

The owner of a dashboard is not automatically the owner of a metric definition, and the engineer who implements a field is not automatically the business authority for its meaning.
