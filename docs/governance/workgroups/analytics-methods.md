# Analytics Methods Workgroup

## Purpose

Improve the consistency, efficiency, and accessibility of data analysis by simplifying and standardizing reusable analytical tools and methods, creating a collaborative forum, and delivering practical resources.

## Primary goals

1. **Simplify and standardize analytics**
2. **Create a collaborative analytics forum**
3. **Develop accessible reusable tools**

## In scope

- descriptive, diagnostic, exploratory, and improvement-oriented methods
- reusable templates and analytical assets
- shared conventions
- enablement and training

## Out of scope

- enterprise privacy or security policy ownership
- enterprise architecture ownership
- project-specific analysis that cannot reasonably be generalized

## Guiding principles

- Practical over perfect
- Consistency with flexibility
- Accessibility by design
- Continuous improvement
