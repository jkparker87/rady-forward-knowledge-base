# Product Management & Personas

This governance area supports consistent, user-centered analytics and digital product development.

## Core responsibilities

- maintain product-development guidance
- maintain validated user personas
- standardize user stories and acceptance criteria
- improve alignment between product functionality and user needs
- reduce rework caused by unclear requirements
- support training and adoption of product-management practices

## Key artifacts

- product lifecycle guidance
- user personas
- user-story framework
- acceptance-criteria patterns
- training materials
