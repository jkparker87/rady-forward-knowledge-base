# AI Literacy

AI literacy should help users understand where AI is useful, how to formulate effective requests, how to evaluate output, and where human judgment remains necessary.

## Suggested curriculum domains

1. What modern AI systems can and cannot do
2. Prompting as problem definition
3. Supplying useful context
4. Grounding and source verification
5. Evaluating AI-generated outputs
6. Privacy, security, and approved-use boundaries
7. AI-assisted analysis and coding
8. Automation versus decision-making
9. Bias, uncertainty, and hallucination
10. Responsible adoption in real workflows
