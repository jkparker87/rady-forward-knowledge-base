# Data Literacy

Data literacy is the ability to ask, understand, evaluate, and communicate with data appropriately.

## Suggested curriculum domains

1. Asking answerable questions
2. Understanding measures, dimensions, grain, and denominators
3. Reading visualizations
4. Variation and uncertainty
5. Comparing groups appropriately
6. Correlation, causation, and confounding
7. Data quality and limitations
8. Choosing appropriate analytical methods
9. Interpreting results for decisions
10. Communicating findings responsibly

Tool-specific training should support these capabilities rather than become the curriculum itself.
